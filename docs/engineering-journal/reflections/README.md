# Reflections

Use this folder for periodic reviews of Astra as an engineering system rather than for individual implementation steps.

A reflection can be written after a major milestone, at the end of a month or quarter, after a failed design, or when the direction of the lab changes.

## Suggested reflection structure

```markdown
# YYYY-MM — Astra engineering reflection

## What changed?

Summarise the important engineering work completed during the period.

## What evidence is strongest?

Which tests or artefacts demonstrate real capability rather than planned work?

## What did not work?

Record failed experiments, abandoned approaches and unresolved problems.

## What did I learn?

Focus on engineering understanding rather than a list of products used.

## What would I design differently?

Identify technical debt, unnecessary complexity or assumptions that should be revisited.

## Skills developed

Examples: networking, Azure, Linux, Windows, identity, automation, IaC, monitoring, security, resilience, troubleshooting.

## Potential research questions

Record questions that have emerged from the work. Do not force every workstream into an academic project.

## Next engineering priority

Choose one or two bounded next steps.
```

Periodic reflection should help keep Astra aligned with infrastructure engineering outcomes while preventing the lab from becoming a collection of unrelated services.
