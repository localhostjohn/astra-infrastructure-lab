# Research Notes

Use this folder for source-led notes that may influence Astra design or future academic work.

Research notes are not implementation evidence. They should help answer questions such as:

- What problem is the technology intended to solve?
- What alternatives exist?
- What do vendor documentation, standards or credible research say?
- What assumptions or limitations apply?
- Which claims still need to be tested in Astra?

## Suggested note structure

```markdown
# Topic

**Date reviewed:** YYYY-MM-DD
**Related Astra workstream:**

## Question

## Sources

Use direct links and enough bibliographic detail to relocate the source later.

## Key findings

Summarise in your own words. Keep factual source findings separate from personal interpretation.

## Relevance to Astra

## Claims to test

## Limitations / conflicting evidence

## Related ADRs, experiments and journal entries
```

For future dissertation use, retain publication details and avoid relying solely on vendor marketing material when independent or peer-reviewed evidence is available.
